How the Crisis Propagated Through Markets

The 1970s shattered the empirical stability of the traditional Phillips Curve. Exogenous geopolitical oil embargoes triggered simultaneous supply contraction ($\Delta AS < 0$) and imported cost inflation. When institutional monetary authorities accommodated higher energy prices with loose liquidity, rational inflation expectations ($\mathbb{E}[\pi]$) unanchored into an accelerating wage-price spiral.

Central Bank & Institutional Resolution

Paul Volcker redefined monetary credibility by abandoning interest rate targets in favor of strict non-borrowed reserve targeting, driving nominal policy rates to 20% to violently break adaptive expectations.
